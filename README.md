## What Is the Huawei HCSE-Presales-Service Certification?

The Huawei Certified Service Expert (HCSE)-Presales-Service certification validates the knowledge and skills required to position, sell, and expand Huawei enterprise service solutions.

It focuses on service consulting, system integration, industry digital transformation, network integration, IT deployment, disaster recovery, and other enterprise service offerings. Candidates should understand Huawei's service business models and how to develop solutions that address customer business requirements.

This certification is designed primarily for Huawei partner pre-sales engineers who want to strengthen their service solution expertise and improve their ability to support enterprise digital transformation projects.
